How to play

Five puzzles a day. Each puzzle shows you search completions — the kind that appear when you start typing into a search bar. Your job: figure out which trending entity produced them.

How it works

  1. You see 2 completions to start. Below them, 8 candidate cards — one is the answer, seven are real distractors.
  2. Tap a card to guess. Wrong guesses dim the card and stay visible — they cost no points.
  3. Stuck? Tap "Reveal another" to surface the next completion. Each reveal lowers your max score for that puzzle by 1 point.
  4. Up to 7 completions can be revealed. After the 7th, the button becomes "Reveal answer" — tapping it gives 0 points.
